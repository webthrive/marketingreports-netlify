What Makes a Good Shopify Dashboard Template?

Good Shopify templates go well past total revenue. They track conversion rate by traffic source, AOV trends, cart abandonment, top-performing products, inventory health, and return rates. The most useful ones blend Shopify with GA4 for traffic funnel analysis and paid media for true ROAS calculation — because ROAS without knowing which channels drove the traffic is just a number that flatters whatever you're already running. One thing to know: Shopify has no native Looker Studio connector. Every template below requires a third-party tool.

Shopify Native Analytics vs Looker Studio Templates

Shopify's built-in analytics are limited: no custom date blending, no cross-channel view, and you can't blend traffic data with sales data. Looker Studio templates fix all of this — but require a connector like Coupler.io, Porter, Supermetrics, or Windsor.ai.

Most connectors charge $15–50/month after their free trial. For stores doing significant revenue, the time savings and cross-channel visibility are worth it within the first week.
